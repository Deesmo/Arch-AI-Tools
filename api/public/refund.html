Refund Policy

Last updated: March 2026

1. Credit Purchases

Arch Tools operates on a pay-per-use credit model. One-time credit purchases are non-refundable once credits have been used. Unused credits from one-time purchases can be refunded within 14 days of purchase.

2. Subscription Plans

Monthly and annual subscriptions can be cancelled at any time. Upon cancellation, you retain access until the end of your current billing period. We do not offer prorated refunds for partial billing periods.

3. x402 (USDC) Payments

Payments made via the x402 protocol using USDC on Base are processed on-chain and are non-refundable due to the nature of blockchain transactions. Each x402 payment covers a single API call at the posted price.

4. Service Errors

If an API call fails due to a server-side error (5xx status) and credits were deducted, contact us within 7 days for a credit restoration. We do not refund for client-side errors (4xx) or upstream provider failures.

5. How to Request a Refund

Email support@archtools.dev with your account email, transaction ID or Stripe receipt, and a description of the issue. We respond within 2 business days.

6. Exceptions

MCMetaverse LLC reserves the right to deny refund requests in cases of suspected abuse, fraud, or violation of the Acceptable Use Policy.
