Changes to Subprocessors

We will update this page when subprocessors are added or removed. Material changes will be communicated to registered users via email with 30 days' notice.

BYOK (Bring Your Own Key)

When you use BYOK headers (e.g. x-anthropic-key, x-openai-key), your API calls are routed directly to the provider using your own credentials. In this case, the provider acts as your direct processor, not as our subprocessor.
